Modal Isles

An archipelago made of text, and the only way to get around is Vim. Each isle teaches a few keys. Clear it by collecting its gems or by reshaping its text until it matches the chart.

Fifteen isles take you from h j k l to :wq. Keys you have not learned yet stay locked, and every isle has a par for keystrokes if you want to chase three stars.

Progress is kept in this browser.
